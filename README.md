 # Phase I – Business Process Modeling
 # Brief description:
 The business process I chose is the one for my project which is injury prevention and football performance management.
In this process, I will aim that it evaluates the physical fitness of players in it, calculate injury risk percentages & establish a transparent, auditable communication loop between the coaching staff, medical department, and performance analysts to protect player workload and improve player performance.
The main actors & their responsibilities in this business process include:
-Head Coach / Technical Staff: Initiates the squad selection process, proposes the match-day lineup, reviews player risk warnings, and submits alternative players if a selection is blocked.
-Performance Analyst: Compiles post-match physical telemetry (minutes played, distance covered, high-intensity sprints) and triggers workload updates in the system.   
-Team Doctor: Conducts physical assessments, logs active injuries and recovery timelines, and approves or denies medical clearance for borderline players.  
-Oracle Database & PL/SQL Engine: Executes fn_calculate_injury_risk, enforces business rules via triggers (blocking injured players), stores validated match lineups, and writes security events to AUDIT_LOG.
-Power BI Analytics Dashboard: Retrieves real-time risk percentages and workload metrics from Oracle to display squad readiness heatmaps for management.
